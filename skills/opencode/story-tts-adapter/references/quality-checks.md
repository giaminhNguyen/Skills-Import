# TTS adaptation quality checks

Check both preservation and listenability.

## Preservation
- Same scene order.
- Same facts and names.
- Same character intentions and knowledge.
- Same reveals and suspense timing.
- Same ending.
- No invented emotions or actions.

## Spoken flow
- Avoid sequences of fragmentary one- or two-word chunks unless dramatically necessary.
- Break long nested sentences at natural semantic boundaries.
- Keep rhetorical repetition when it is intentionally dramatic.
- Do not flatten every sentence to the same length.
- Let dialogue remain recognizably dialogue.
- Use punctuation to guide breath and emphasis only when the engine benefits from it.

## Chunk flow
- Prefer chunk boundaries at paragraph or sentence boundaries.
- Never split inside a name, number, quoted phrase, or tightly bound clause when avoidable.
- Merge tiny trailing chunks when this stays under the hard limit.
