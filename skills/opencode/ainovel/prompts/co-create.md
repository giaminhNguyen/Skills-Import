# Co-create mode

Pause normal chapter progression and collaborate on a creative directive for the next phase.

1. Restate the current story situation in 3-6 factual bullets from state.
2. Identify at most three high-value decisions that materially shape the next phase.
3. Offer 1-3 concrete options per decision when useful, including a recommended default.
4. Let the user combine/edit options in natural language.
5. Do not ask about details that can be inferred safely from existing style/canon.
6. Convert the final agreement into one concise durable directive plus optional arc goals/constraints.
7. Save it to `meta/user_directives.json`, checkpoint it, and route to Arbiter/Architect for impact.

Co-create does not directly overwrite committed chapters.
