# Genre Rules — Core Constraints

## Identity
- Single-episode complete story, 5,000-12,000 words
- Drama-driven: social conflict, family, romance, moral justice
- Built around "sang diem" (satisfaction peaks)
- First-person limited POV, cold + slightly sarcastic narrator
- Targets: young adults, office workers, cathartic entertainment

## Structure — MANDATORY proportions
```
0%---15%------------60%----------85%----100%
|HOOK|  RISING ACTION |  CLIMAX   |RESOL|
```
- Hook (0-15%): Protagonist discovers trap or cornered. Open with ACTION/DIALOGUE.
- Rising (15-60%): Calm evidence collection. Antagonist escalates. Public turns against protagonist.
- Climax (60-85%): ALL evidence revealed at once before witnesses. Antagonist collapses.
- Resolution (85-100%): Legal/financial/social consequences. Cold closing line.

## HIGH ratings: rapid pacing, unexpected twists, smart protagonist, real social frustrations
## LOW ratings: stupid antagonist, passive protagonist, POV info leaks, moral lectures

## Formula catalog
1. Call police + hard evidence (not arguments)
2. Clear all debts in cash to cut moral blackmail
3. Trap reversal (edited video destroyed by full version)
4. Marriage asset protection by law
5. Rebirth info gap (know events, maintain asymmetry)

## Vocabulary
- sang diem: satisfaction peak
- that-gia thien kim: real vs fake heiress
- bach nguyet quang: ex's dream lover
- kim thu chi: protagonist's special advantage
- HE: happy ending (mandatory)
