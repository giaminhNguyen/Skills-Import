# Anti-AI Checklist — 15 Rules

## A: Voice & Rhythm (ROLLBACK if violated)
A1: NO scenery-only openings. Open with ACTION or DIALOGUE.
A2: NO >5 consecutive short sentences. Alternate short (3-7 words) with medium (15-25). Ratio 3:7.
A3: NO repeated sentence structures (>3 in a row). Vary subject, structure.
A4: "Viet-Trung" style MANDATORY:
- Sino-Vietnamese names (Hua Chi Giao, NOT Nguyen Van A)
- Address: "cuc cung", "thien kim", "tong giam doc Ha"
- Body: "sac mat cung do", "yet hau lan mot cai"
- NO lyrical Vietnamese prose, NO internet slang

## B: Emotion & Reaction (ROLLBACK if violated)
B1: NO cliche emotional descriptions ("tim nhu bi bop nghet", "con gian trao dang nhu song").
USE: physical action ("Cay but ky bi toi bam gay luon.") or one-word sensation ("lanh").
B2: Crying max 2 sentences. Then action.
B3: NO long explanations from protagonist. Place evidence, stay silent.

## C: Dialogue (ROLLBACK if violated)
C1: NO exposition dump in dialogue.
C2: ALL characters must have distinct voices. Test: remove names, still identifiable?
C3: NO repeated dialogue tags ("anh ta noi" x5). Use action tags or drop tags.

## D: Structure & Pacing (WARNING level)
D1: Paragraphs <=150 words.
D2: NO template transitions ("Thoi gian troi qua..."). Jump-cut with timestamps.
D3: SHOW via scenes, don't summarize events.
D4: NO moral lecture endings. End with cold line or quiet action.

## E: Final Check (Review step)
E1: Count AI keywords ("khong khoi", "bong nhien nhan ra", "tu do ve sau", "cam giac am ap lan toa"). If >3 total -> fix.
E2: Dialogue ratio 40-55%. If outside -> adjust.
E3: Sang Diem Test: mark moments that make you want to applaud. If <4 -> add more.
