Each story must use 3-5 types. Evidence must be PLANTED/DISCOVERED within story.

A: Recording — 1.Hidden camera 2.Phone recording 3.Livestream capture 4.Body cam 5.Failed message recall
B: Financial — 6.Bank statements 7.Transfer records with notes 8.Receipts 9.Debt IOUs 10.Property records
C: Official — 11.Forensic test results 12.Student records 13.Medical records 14.Registration data 15.Restored chat logs

Integration:
- Rising: collect 2-3 types (show the ACT)
- Climax: reveal ALL AT ONCE publicly. Pattern: deny->evidence->deny->stronger evidence->collapse
- Final piece = something antagonist thought was destroyed
