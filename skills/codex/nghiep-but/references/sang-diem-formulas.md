# Sang Diem Formulas — 12 Types

Phase 1 (15-30%):
F1 Quiet Refusal — protagonist calmly refuses what everyone expects them to accept.
F2 First Clue — protagonist notices tiny detail others miss, proves they're 10 steps ahead.

Phase 2 (30-55%):
F3 Reversal Question — one question flips power dynamic.
F4 Numeric Punch — specific number destroys antagonist's narrative (80,000 vs 800).
F5 Silent Departure — protagonist leaves without arguing. Antagonist panics.

Phase 3 (55-75%):
F6 Public Unmasking — evidence revealed before audience.
F7 Ally Revelation — powerful person unexpectedly connected to protagonist. AFTER 60% only.
F8 Double Betrayal — antagonist's own ally turns on them.

Phase 4 (75-90%):
F9 Document Destruction — physically destroy antagonist's power source (rip contract).
F10 Institutional Verdict — official judgment with specific sentence.
F11 Mirror Moment — antagonist forced into exact position they put protagonist in.

Ending (90-100%):
F12 Cold Close — one line <=15 words carrying weight of entire story. No explanation after.
