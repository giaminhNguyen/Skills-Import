# Character Archetypes — 7 Templates

1. PROTAGONIST: Rational, decisive, fair about money. Calls police, records evidence, cuts ties. Voice: calm, sarcastic. NEVER begs.
2. SCUM HUSBAND: Spineless, enables abusers, schemes for wife's assets. Fake tenderness + cold calculation.
3. WHITE MOONLIGHT (bach nguyet quang): Appears fragile, actually mastermind. Cries on cue, redirects blame. Public=victim, Private=operator.
4. TOXIC ELDER: Uses "tradition" to control. Moral blackmail. "Lam nguoi dung tuyet tinh qua."
5. PROTECTOR (tong tai): Powerful, supports protagonist unconditionally. Appears AFTER 60% — protagonist fights alone first.
6. JUSTICE PILLAR (police/lawyer/doctor): Professional, neutral, validates evidence. Makes victory official.
7. TOXIC SIBLING: Parasitic, entitled, steals resources while playing victim.
