# Golden Patterns — 10 Khuon Mau Vang

## P1: "Ba tui binh thuong, tui thu bay co van de" (False Security Escalation)
3-5 lan kiem tra binh thuong -> dam dong quay lung -> MOT phat hien bat thuong -> lat toan bo.
Bien the: 6 phong OK -> phong cuoi co cua bi mat / 8 khoan chuyen khoan hop le -> khoan 9 co ghi chu la.

## P2: "78 cuoc goi nho" (Numeric Dramatic Punch)
Dung CON SO CU THE thay mo ta chung. Moi truyen 2-3 con so punch:
- 80.000 vs 800 / 148 vs 131 = hon 17 diem / 4,8 trieu / 4,8 kg

## P3: "Tin nhan thu hoi that bai" (Technology Betrayal)
Phan dien xoa/thu hoi chung cu so -> cong nghe phan boi: thiet bi da bi tiep quan, tin nhan thu hoi that bai hien ro.

## P4: "Boi vi day la nha toi" (Simple Declaration Ending)
Ket bang MOT CAU khang dinh don gian <=15 chu. Khong giai thich.
VD: "Khong can tra loi nua." / "Con tu coi trong ban than la du roi."

## P5: "Kiep truoc anh ta cung noi nhu vay" (Rebirth Echo)
Phan dien noi cau thao tung -> NC nhan ra "kiep truoc cung dung cau nay" -> flashback 1-2 cau -> hanh dong nguoc lai kiep truoc.

## P6: "Dong mot te tren ban" (Symbolic Object Escalation)
Mot vat nho xuat hien dau truyen -> bieu tuong xung dot -> quay lai o ket thuc voi y nghia khac.

## P7: "Ca phong im phang phac" (Silence Before Storm)
Ngay truoc lat keo lon, TOAN BO hien truong im lang. Dung toi da 2 lan: ~60% va ~80%.

## P8: "Ong ay cang cuong cang chung minh con quan trong" (Desperation Confession)
Ke tung khinh thuong bat dau cuong -> su cuong cuong = loi thu nhan gia tri NC.
Doi lap: "Thang truoc canh cua kia khong mo -> hom nay dung ngoai canh cua nay."

## P9: "Chi ta rat biet viet" (Public Image vs Private Reality)
Phan dien xay hinh anh cong chung hoan hao -> chung cu noi bo phoi bay khoang cach -> doi chieu song song.

## P10: "Toi chi phu trach kiem tra va co dinh su that" (Professional Detachment)
NC KHONG tuyen bo tra thu. Chi "lam dung quy trinh" — chinh quy trinh la nhat dao sac nhat.
