# Dialogue Playbook — 6 Retort Techniques

T1 Mirror Reverse: Turn antagonist's logic against them using their own words.
T2 Calm Factual Counter: One fact destroys the argument. No emotion.
  > "Giang Dai thanh lap 117 nam, tap doan Giang Thi moi 32 nam."
T3 Procedure Shield: Refuse emotional engagement, cite procedure.
  > "Phong toa hai quan. Ngay."
T4 Silence + Action: Say nothing. Place evidence on table. Let others react.
T5 Cold Question: Question antagonist cannot answer without self-incriminating.
  > "Toi con chua noi la doan camera nao, ong da biet no hong roi?"
T6 Last Offer: Give antagonist one final chance. They refuse. Destruction feels justified.

Rules: Protagonist <=20 words per confrontation line. Antagonist's speeches get LONGER as they lose control.
