# Style Snippets — giong van Viet-Trung (mau minh hoa, viet moi)

Cac doan duoi day duoc viet moi de minh hoa TUNG KY THUAT.
Agent hoc GIONG DIEU va NHIP, khong sao chep.

## 1. Mo dau bang hanh dong/doi thoai (khong ta canh)  [rule A1]
> Ngay toi ky ten vao don ly hon, me chong dat len ban mot cai tui.
> "Con dau, kiem tra lai di. Trong nha minh khong ai an trom ca."
> Toi mo tui ra. Mot chiec vong vang nam giua dong quan ao cu.

## 2. Nen cam xuc bang hanh dong (khong ta "tim dau nhoi")  [rule B1]
> Toi doc het tin nhan. Dau ngon tay hoi lanh.
> Coc nuoc tren ban van con am. Toi uong mot ngum, dat xuong, khoa man hinh.

## 3. Retort — cau hoi vach mat  [dialogue T5]
> "Camera hong roi." Truong phong cong tac sinh vien lanh mat.
> Toi nhin chiec camera dang xoay tren dau. "Toi con chua noi la doan nao,
> sao ong da biet no hong?"

## 4. Retort — doi dau bang su that  [dialogue T2]
> "Truong nay do nha toi bo tien xay."
> "Truong thanh lap 117 nam roi. Tap doan nha co moi 32 nam. Co cung dam noi
> truong nay do nha co xay sao?"

## 5. Im lang truoc bao to  [pattern P7]
> Toi dat tung to giay len ban. Sao ke. Ghi am. Giay no viet tay.
> Ca phong im phang phac.

## 6. Numeric punch  [pattern P2 / F4]
> "Moi thang bo me chuyen tam muoi trieu."
> Toi ngang dau. "Nhung moi thang con nhan duoc, chi co tam tram."

## 7. Cau ket "lanh"  [pattern P4 / F12]
> Toi thang day an toan.
> "Sau nay chac co ta se khong den nua."
>
> (hoac)
>
> Toi nhin dong chu ay vai giay, roi dat dien thoai xuong.
> Khong can tra loi nua.

## 8. Nhip cau ngan-dai xen ke 3:7  [rule A2]
> Toi khong nhuc nhich. Sac mat co ta cung lai, xung quanh co nguoi khong nhin
> duoc bat cuoi, nhung khong ai dam len tieng truoc anh mat cua co ta.

## 9. Giong ke lanh + cham biem nhe  [protagonist voice]
> Co ta van cho rang tat ca nhung gi minh tung co deu bi toi cuop mat.
> Toi cung phai kham phuc. Dien den muc nay ma van tin duoc nhu vay.
