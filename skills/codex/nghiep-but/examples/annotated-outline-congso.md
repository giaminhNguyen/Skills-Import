# Annotated Outline — Cong So (Freeloader Escalation)

Minh hoa mau "paper trail": tich luy tai lieu, xu ly o HR khong lam canh cong khai on ao.

## Concept
- Setting: Cong so | Motif: M1 (freeloader escalation) + M3 (manager complicity)
- Cold closing line: "Muon an mien phi thi truoc het hay di lam mien phi."

## Phase 1 — HOOK (0-15%)
- Opening: dong nghiep an het do an vat nhap khau cua NC, dat "dong 1 te"
  len ban roi doi NC cam on.  [Mo dau bang XUNG DOT + vat bieu tuong P6]
- Sang diem F1 (Quiet Refusal): NC day dong 1 te tra lai.

## Phase 2 — RISING (15-60%)
- Antagonist escalation (leo thang tung nac):

- Chung cu (paper trail): anh chup, ma nhan tien, phieu xuat kho hang can han,
  loi xac nhan cua lao cong, ghi am "dong 1 te".

## Phase 3 — CLIMAX (60-85%)
- Trigger: chi gai doi phuong keo camera toi cong ty lam am -> bi lat nguoc.
- Sang diem F6 (Public Unmasking): mo ghi am + phieu xuat kho truoc HR.
- Sang diem F8 (Double Betrayal): chi gai doi phuong quay sang trach em.
- Manager (dong loa) cung bi loi ra.

## Phase 4 — RESOLUTION (85-100%)
- F10 (Verdict): dong nghiep bi cham dut thu viec, quan ly bi ghi loi.
- P6 payoff: dong 1 te nem vao may ban hang -> "so du khong du".
- Cold close F12: "Muon an mien phi thi truoc het hay di lam mien phi."
