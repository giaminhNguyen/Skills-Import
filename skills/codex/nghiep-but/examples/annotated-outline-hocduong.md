# Annotated Outline — Hoc Duong (Silent Transfer)

Minh hoa mau "thang bang cach roi di", it bao luc, sang bang su diem tinh.

## Concept
- Setting: Hoc duong | Motif: M1 (stolen slot)
- Cold closing line: "Con tu coi trong ban than la du roi."

## Phase 1 — HOOK (0-15%)
- Opening: phu huynh doc bang thong bao 3 lan — con minh (nhat toan quan)
  bi gach ten khoi doi tuyen, thay bang hoc sinh la (~70 diem).  [Mo dau bang HANH DONG]
- Injustice cu the: "nguoi dung nhat bi gach, nguoi thu 273 duoc vao".

## Phase 2 — RISING (15-60%)
- Sang diem F5 (Silent Departure): KHONG lam am o nhom phu huynh,
  chi lang le chuyen truong.  [DAY la sang diem trung tam cua the loai nay]
- Antagonist escalation:
  1. Hieu truong cu goi 78 cuoc (P2 - Numeric Punch)
  2. Me hoc sinh la mang qua toi nha nho va
  3. Truong cu tung tin don "phu huynh gay chuyen"
- Chung cu: bang diem, giay chung nhan giai, thu hang cap quan (giay trang muc den).

## Phase 3 — CLIMAX (60-85%)
- Trigger: hieu truong + me hoc sinh la den tan cua xin.
- Sang diem F4 (Numeric Punch): con so 273 vach tran "danh gia tong hop".
- Golden pattern P8 (Desperation Confession):
  "Thang truoc neu ong chiu mo canh cua kia..."
- Ket qua thi: 148/150, hon nguoi thu hai 17 diem.

## Phase 4 — RESOLUTION (85-100%)
- Truong cu mat thanh tich, hoc sinh la bi phat hien chep bai.
- Con gai: "Nguoi khac co coi trong con hay khong khong con quan trong."
- Cold close F12: "Con tu coi trong ban than la du roi." (con noi) /
  hoac ket bang tin nhan "chuc mung" khong tra loi.
