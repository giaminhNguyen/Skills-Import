# Setting: Hon Nhan & Gia Dinh (Marriage & Family)

Core conflict: Financial exploitation, emotional abuse, or identity fraud within family.

Motifs:
- M1 Asset Trap: spouse secretly consults lawyers to claim pre-marriage assets. Evidence: consultation records, transfer attempts, prenups.
- M2 Mother-in-law Test: plants expensive items (gold rings) to frame theft, or buys tainted food. Evidence: store receipt, CCTV of planting.
- M3 Sibling Money Theft: sibling skims living allowance. Parents give X, sibling forwards 1/100th. Evidence: bank records full amount vs received. (Sample: 80,000 -> 800)
- M4 Surrogacy Coercion: family pressures protagonist to carry child for relative.

Evidence: bank statements (mismatch), audio recordings, medical records, handwritten IOUs, chat logs with timestamps.
