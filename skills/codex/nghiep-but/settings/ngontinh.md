# Setting: Ngon Tinh (Urban Romance / CEO Romance)

Core conflict: Protagonist in relationship with powerful man, faces threat from jealous ex or scheming rival. Romance wrapped around protection/conspiracy plot.

Motifs:
- M1 Pregnancy Trap: protagonist pregnant, partner claims not wanting kids. Actually rival plots to force abortion or frame protagonist. Twist: partner already knows, angry she didn't tell him.
- M2 Money vs Love: rival offers money to leave. Key line: "Truoc khi anh chan em, em se khong roi di."
- M3 Protector Who Knows Everything: male lead appears lazy/casual but protects from shadows. Power revealed gradually.

Tone: MORE internal monologue, MORE intimacy (tasteful), LESS institutional resolution. Satisfaction = emotional revelation, not legal victory.
Male lead: teasing + deadly serious. "cuc cung"/"bao boi". Female: more vulnerable but has backbone.
