# Setting: Ly Ky / Thriller (Mystery / Attic Secret / Smuggling)

Core conflict: Protagonist discovers dangerous secret and must outmaneuver perpetrators.

Motifs:
- M1 Attic Parasite: previous owner's family secretly living in attic. Protagonist reborn -> sets trap. Uses hidden cameras. Forces them to reveal by threatening renovation.
- M2 Customs/Smuggling: protagonist is border officer. Ex asks favor to pass goods. Previous life agreed -> framed. This life: full inspection. Evidence layers: surface normal -> deep abnormal -> warehouse labels -> chat logs -> transfers.
- M3 Property Invasion: someone renovates house without permission. Deliberate strategy to force a sale. Evidence: forged authorization, cut-paste signature, recorded instructions.

Rules: slower build-up, faster revelation. Protagonist smarter than reader. Camera POV shifts. Physical details matter (weight of bag, powder color, seal marks).
