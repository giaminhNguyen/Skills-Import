# Setting: Hoc Duong (School / Academic Competition)

Core conflict: Academic opportunity stolen through connections. Protagonist proves worth through undeniable results.

Motifs:
- M1 Stolen Slot: competition/exam slot given to connected student. Protagonist doesn't complain — quietly transfers. Original school panics losing top performer.
- M2 Fake Identity Exposure: someone claims elite school/background they don't have. Evidence: registration records, school databases, physical visit to claimed home.

Key pattern — The Silent Transfer:
1. Discover injustice -> don't argue
2. Transfer quietly -> no social media
3. Excel at new school -> results speak
4. Old school metrics collapse -> they come begging
5. Protagonist: silence or brief factual answer

Evidence: exam scores/rankings (specific numbers), student records, competition results, viral exposure videos.
Emotional core: parent-child. Parent's quiet determination. Child says something too wise for their age.
