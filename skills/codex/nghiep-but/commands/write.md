# Step 4: Write

## Before executing
Read: references/anti-ai-checklist.md + references/evidence-arsenal.md + references/dialogue-playbook.md

## Writing sequence
Write in order: Hook -> Rising -> Climax -> Resolution. Do NOT skip ahead.

## POV rules
- First person limited ("toi"). Only what protagonist sees/hears/feels/thinks.
- Others' emotions shown through ACTIONS and DIALOGUE only.
- Inner thoughts: short, dry, occasionally sarcastic.

## The "Viet-Trung" voice (SIGNATURE of the genre)
- Body cues: "sac mat cung do", "yet hau lan mot cai", "dau ngon tay hoi te dai"
- Emotional compression: action = emotion ("Cay but ky trong tay toi bi toi bam gay luon.")
- Crowd silence: "Ca hien truong im phang phac."
- Cold narrator asides: dry observations, no melodrama

## Sentence rhythm
Alternate short (3-7 words) and medium (15-25 words). Ratio 3:7.

## Pacing
- NO scenery-only paragraphs
- Paragraph max: 5 sentences / 150 words
- Dialogue ratio: 40-55%
- Scene transitions: time-stamp jump cuts ("Ba ngay sau.", "10:00 sang.")

## Evidence revelation during climax
1. Weakest evidence -> antagonist dismisses
2. Medium evidence -> nervous but argues
3. Strong evidence -> panics
4. Devastating evidence -> collapses
5. (Optional) Antagonist's own ally betrays them

## Verify against anti-ai-checklist.md before completing each phase.

## Word count targets
- After Hook: ~15% of target
- After Rising: ~60%
- After Climax: ~85%
- After Resolution: 100% (5,000-12,000)
